# Privacy Policy for VaraMc

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines what information **VaraMc** ("the Bot") accesses, how it is used, and how your data is protected within our Discord server community.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
The Bot accesses only the minimum necessary data required to operate server moderation and ticketing features:
- **Discord User Data:** User IDs, usernames, and avatars (to identify ticket creators and manage permissions).
- **Server Data:** Guild IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs (to configure private ticket channels and manage permissions).
- **Message Content:** Accessed strictly in real time to filter spam, detect blacklisted words/links, and generate support transcripts when a private ticket is resolved.
- **Presence & Status Data:** Monitored exclusively for active staff members to coordinate live support availability.

### 2. How We Use the Information
Collected data is used strictly for:
- Creating and routing support tickets to the appropriate staff teams.
- Generating closed-ticket conversation transcripts for community audit and quality assurance.
- Automated moderation (AutoMod) to detect rule violations, phishing, and spam.
- Assigning appropriate roles and managing channel visibility.

### 3. Data Storage & Retention
- We **do not sell, rent, or trade** any user data to third parties.
- Message content is evaluated in-memory in real time and is not permanently stored, except for archived ticket transcripts retained securely for moderation review.
- Temporary logs and cached data are routinely purged to ensure minimal data retention.

### 4. Third-Party Sharing
Data processed by the Bot is not shared with any external companies or marketing third parties. All interactions stay within the Discord infrastructure.

### 5. Data Deletion & User Rights
Users have the right to request the deletion of their ticket history and associated transcripts. If you wish to have your stored ticket transcripts removed, please reach out to our administration team directly on Discord.

### 6. Policy Updates
This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect new features or API adjustments. Changes take effect upon posting to this repository.

---

### 7. Contact Us
For any inquiries or requests concerning your data and privacy, please reach out via a support ticket on our official Discord server.
